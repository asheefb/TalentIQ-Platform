RAG - (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

Goal 
* Make AI answer using your data (resume, docs)
* Not just generic ChatGPT answers

How Rag Works
####
    1. Upload document
       2. Chunk document
       3. Generate embeddings
       4. Store embeddings
       5. Generate question embedding
       6. Calculate similarity
       7. Retrieve top chunks
       8. Send context to LLM
       9. Generate grounded response
